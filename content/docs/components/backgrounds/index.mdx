Fullscreen WebGPU background shaders adapted from [Paper Shaders](https://github.com/paper-design/shaders) (`Apache-2.0`) and ported to TypeGPU for `shadercn`.

- **[Warp](/docs/components/backgrounds/warp)** — Animated color fields warped by value noise and layered swirls over checks, stripes, or split edge patterns.
- **[Water](/docs/components/backgrounds/water)** — Water-like surface distortion with natural dual-layer caustic realism and simplex wave refraction.
